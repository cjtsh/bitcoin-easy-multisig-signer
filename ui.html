3. Prepare a send

?
What is a PSBT?

PSBT means Partially Signed Bitcoin Transaction. Here, it is an unsigned file describing a proposed payment for your signers to review and approve. Preparing it does not move Bitcoin.

Prepare an unsigned transaction file for your signers. Nothing is signed or sent here.

Transaction speed

?
What does the fee rate mean?

The network fee pays for a transaction to be included on the network. sat/vB measures the fee per unit of transaction size. A higher rate can help it confirm sooner, but timing is not guaranteed.

Choose a speed. Review the fee before saving.

Advanced: adjust network fee

This controls how much is paid to the Bitcoin network, not the amount sent. The selected rate is limited to 1–25 sat/vB and the transaction fee to 10,000 sats. Final effective sat/vB can vary slightly with signer signature sizes.

Only confirmed Bitcoin can be sent. Check the amount and fee on your signer.

Where does the rest go?

Bitcoin spends whole outputs. On a partial send, the amount not sent or paid as a fee returns to an address in your wallet. Check that change address on your signer.
