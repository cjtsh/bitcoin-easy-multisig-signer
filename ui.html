3. Check your hardware wallets

?
Why check them first?

This wallet needs more than one device before any Bitcoin can move, and none of your keys are in this app. Checking the devices here means a missing or mismatched one is found calmly, now, instead of at the last step of a payment.

Your wallet is a . Spending needs more than one of those devices, so check now that they are here and that they belong to this wallet — before you build a payment.

    If no device appears, check these in order:

    • Unlock the device with its PIN on the device itself, then look again.
    • Open the device's Bitcoin app, if it has one. A Ledger needs it open.
    • Use a data cable. Some cables only carry power.
    • Close any other wallet software — it can hold the device open.
    • Try another USB port, without a hub between the device and the Mac.

    This reads each device's public identity only. Nothing is signed, nothing is sent, and any PIN is entered on the device itself — never here.

    What is a PSBT?

    PSBT means Partially Signed Bitcoin Transaction. Here, it is an unsigned file describing a proposed payment for your signers to review and approve. Preparing it does not move Bitcoin.

    First prepare and review the transaction. Nothing is signed or sent here.

    Would you like to prepare a Bitcoin transaction?

    You will enter the destination and amount, review the fee and change, then save an unsigned file. This app will not sign or send Bitcoin.

    Recommended: send a small test amount first. Confirm that a small payment arrives before you move a large amount. Nothing here is forced — a test transaction is simply the cheapest insurance there is.

    What does the fee rate mean?

    The network fee pays for a transaction to be included on the network. sat/vB measures the fee per unit of transaction size. A higher rate can help it confirm sooner, but timing is not guaranteed.
